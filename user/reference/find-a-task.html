On this page

Find a task ​

Audience: Anyone who knows what they want to do but not what Manager calls it.

Manager's pages are named after what a feature is: First control, Faulty, Changes report. You usually know what you want to do: "start the course from a normal control", "a box died", "get the new card numbers into next week". This page lists tasks under the ways people ask for them and links straight to the section that answers each one.

Each entry starts with the task, then other ways it's often asked (also), then the section that covers it. If your task isn't here, try the search box at the top of the page, or ask the built-in assistant.
