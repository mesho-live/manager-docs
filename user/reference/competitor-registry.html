Competitor Registry ​

Audience: Event organisers maintaining a cross-event list of known competitors.

The Competitor Registry is meshO Manager's master list of the people who take part across your events — their names, clubs, card numbers, Eventor/IOF identifiers, and birth year and sex. Once someone is in the registry they're recognised from one event to the next, so entering them again is a matter of picking their name rather than re-typing every detail.

It's one of the pages under Settings → Global registry, alongside the Club Registry and the Hire card registry. Everything here is global — shared by every event on this server, and kept separate from any single event's competitor list.

What the registry does for you ​

  • Faster entry. When you add a competitor to an event, typing a name searches the registry; picking a match fills in their club, card number, Eventor/IOF IDs, and birth year and sex for you. An entry list or start list you import is topped up the same way, wherever it leaves a person's birth year or sex blank. See Competitors, clubs and teams.
  • Where the people come from. From Eventor — Refresh competitors from Eventor, and by itself after an Eventor import — from ANNE, the Austrian orienteering portal — Refresh competitors from ANNE — and from anyone you add by hand. See Keeping it up to date with Eventor, Filling it from ANNE and Add and edit people.
  • Recognising cards. When a card is downloaded that isn't entered in the current event, Manager looks the number up in the registry and shows the likely owner's name (in brackets) so you can add them in one click. See Downloading cards.

Open the Competitor Registry ​

In Settings, under Global registry, choose Competitor registry. The count beside the search box shows how many people it holds.

What's in the list ​

Each row is one person, listed alphabetically by name, 50 to a page:

ColumnNotes
External IDThe person's number in an entry system such as Eventor. Blank for someone you added by hand without one.
ProviderThe system that number belongs to — usually Eventor. The same number can belong to two different people in two different systems, so the registry tells them apart by the pair.
NameClick it to edit the person.
ClubTheir club, drawn from the Club Registry.
Card numberTheir SportIdent or EMIT card number, if known.
IOF IDThe athlete's IOF Person ID, used by exports and re-imports.

Your custom fields, if you have any, follow as extra columns. Every column is shown to begin with: hide the ones you don't use from the column menu, drag a heading by its grip to reorder, and click a heading to sort. The layout is remembered on this computer.

Use Search competitors to filter by name, club, or card number.

Birth year and sex ​

The registry keeps each person's birth year and sex, but they aren't columns in the list: they are private, and the list can be read by anyone on your network. You see them on the person's Edit competitor dialog instead. If your registry holds an Eventor or ANNE refresh, set a PIN under Settings → Access protection; without one, anyone on your network can read them too.

  • Where they come from. An Eventor or ANNE refresh fills them in for everyone it lists (Manager keeps only the year of a birth date). For someone you added by hand, type them in on Edit competitor.
  • An Eventor refresh replaces them for Eventor's people, including values you typed. Correct them in Eventor itself. People you added by hand keep what you typed.
  • The event's own values win. When you add a registry person to an event, or import an entry that names them, their birth year and sex fill only what the entry leaves blank. A competitor who already has a birth year or sex in the event keeps it, and changing a competitor in an event never changes the registry.

If a new competitor's birth year and sex stay blank, either the registry doesn't hold them for that person, or you typed the name instead of picking the person from the list.

Note: Rankings are not kept here. They belong to one event, not to the person, because a ranking list is republished every few weeks — a place remembered from an event three months ago would be quietly out of date rather than obviously missing. Import the current list into each event that needs one; see Importing rankings.

Add and edit people ​

From the Actions menu:

  • Add competitor… — a Surname is required; First name, Card number, IOF ID, Birth year, Sex, Club, External ID and ID provider are optional, as are any custom fields. An External ID can't be one that another person already holds under the same provider.
  • Clear competitor registry… — removes every person after you confirm, including people you added by hand and IOF IDs recorded only here. Events keep their own competitors. This can't be undone; with Eventor switched on, Refresh competitors from Eventor fills it again.

Click a person's name to open Edit competitor, where you can change any of their details or delete them. To fill Card number, click into the field and insert the card into a connected reader.

Changes here only reach events you create or enter people into afterwards — a competitor already in an event isn't changed, and deleting someone from the registry doesn't affect any event.

An event where cards are routinely borrowed can choose not to use the registry at all — see Ignore competitor registry. The registry itself is untouched, and every other event goes on using it.

Keeping it up to date with Eventor ​

Today the registry is populated from Eventor. From the Actions menu, Refresh competitors from Eventor pulls the latest people for the Organisation ID in your Eventor settings and reports how many were loaded. The item only appears once Enable Eventor is switched on and the connection is set up under Settings → Eventor (see Settings).

Manager also refreshes the registry by itself, in the background, after you import an event or its entries from Eventor — at most once a day.

What a refresh changes: it adds people who are new in Eventor and updates the details (name, club, card number, birth year and sex) of people already in the registry — it never deletes. People you added by hand, and anyone Eventor no longer lists, are kept. Two things to know: a hand edit to an Eventor person's name, club, card, birth year or sex is overwritten on the next refresh (make lasting changes in Eventor itself), and your IOF ID and custom-field values are always preserved, because Eventor's export doesn't include them.

Filling it from ANNE ​

With ANNE switched on under Settings → ANNE, the Actions menu has Refresh competitors from ANNE. Manager reads people from one of two places, and the message afterwards says which:

  • ANNE's people list — everyone ANNE holds. Only some ANNE accounts may read it, so this needs an API key from such an account.
  • The entries of the last year's ANNE events — used when there is no API key, or ANNE won't list people for yours. Everyone who entered a completed ANNE event in the last year, with the card and club from their most recent entry. Reading a year of events takes about a minute. People entered without an ANNE account aren't added.

Like an Eventor refresh, it adds and updates and never deletes. Each person's club is added to the Club registry if it isn't there already. A person in the registry from ANNE is matched to the same person in an entry list imported from ANNE, so their birth year, sex and custom fields come with them.
